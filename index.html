About me

I wear several hats: trainer, teacher, value-chain analyst, planner, evaluator and writer. Over 22 years I have worked with state missions, NGOs, research institutes and my own firm. I founded KPS India Pvt. Ltd. and ran it as Managing Director from 2017 to 2024, delivering surveys and studies for international research centres, donors and CSR programmes. I am a Grade-A National Resource Person for DAY-NRLM, an empanelled trainer at NIRD&PR, and Visiting Faculty at XIM University, Bhubaneswar.

My work moves between the field, the training hall, the classroom and the desk: a focus group in a village, a value-chain map, a cash-flow model, a session for community cadres or a journal paper. I have worked in India, the USA, Kenya, Bangladesh, Nepal, Thailand and the Philippines.

Languages: Odia (mother tongue), English and Hindi (fluent), Bengali (fair).

Tools: KoboToolbox, SPSS, STATA, advanced Excel, QGIS and ArcGIS.
